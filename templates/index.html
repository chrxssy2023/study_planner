Welcome to Your Study Planner

This is a Study Planner called Studyous. In this website, you are able to study organised, manage your workload, and keep track of your study goals all in one place. Your Study Planner is here to also help you manage assignments, subjects, notes and deadlines so you can stay on top of your schoolwork with reminders to help you remember important information you might have forgotten!


Features

Smart Search: Find subjects and assignments quickly without having to scroll through the website.

Sorting: Sort subjects and assignments by priority, due date, status, credits, room or teacher to make it easier to find important information.

Colour Modes: Choose from four colour modes: Pink, Light, Dark and Colourblind.
